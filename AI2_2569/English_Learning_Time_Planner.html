5. Calculation and assumptions

D = H / (30 × M × W / 7)

D = hours per study day; H = estimated guided learning hours; M = months; W = study days per week. One month is approximated as 30 days. Actual study days may vary with the calendar.

Example: B1 → B2 requires an estimated 180 hours. Over 2.5 months with daily study: 180 / 75 = 2.4 hours (2 hours 24 minutes).

Planning assumptions for successive level transitions: Beginner → A1: 95 hours; A1 → A2: 95; A2 → B1: 180; B1 → B2: 180; B2 → C1: 200; C1 → C2: 250. Larger transitions add these estimates together.

Important: Guided learning hours are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes or a direct measure of independent study hours. Practice quality, feedback, prior knowledge and consistency affect progress. Assess each skill regularly and revise your plan.
